How it works

About Ancestral X HOF

This is a community Hall of Fame for players who have completed Pokémon Ancestral X. All Credit goes to Axyn for Ancestral X.

Getting inducted

  • Play the game as intended and complete it as either a Nuzlocke or Hardcore Nuzlocke..
  • Ping @RWXO in the Ancestral X Discord with your version, attempts, deaths, and an Elite Four run link when available.
